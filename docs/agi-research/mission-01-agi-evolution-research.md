# MISSION 01 — AGI EVOLUTION RESEARCH

Conduct an advanced research investigation into the fundamental limitations of current AI systems.

Your objective is to identify capabilities that DARK AGI does not currently possess and discover practical architectures that could overcome these limitations.

Investigate:

1. Reasoning limitations.
2. Long-term memory limitations.
3. Continual learning limitations.
4. Long-horizon planning.
5. Cross-domain knowledge transfer.
6. Autonomous problem decomposition.
7. Self-evaluation and error correction.
8. Multi-agent coordination.
9. Scientific reasoning.
10. Novel algorithm discovery.

Do not simply list existing AI technologies.

Identify specific limitations, explain why they exist, investigate possible solutions, and propose new engineering approaches.

For each proposed capability:

- Explain the limitation.
- Research existing approaches.
- Identify their weaknesses.
- Propose an improved architecture.
- Explain the computational requirements.
- Design a working prototype.
- Define measurable benchmarks.
- Compare against a baseline.

Inspect the existing DARK AGI architecture before proposing implementation.

Prioritize capabilities that can be built and tested in the current project.

Deliver a ranked AGI Evolution Roadmap with the highest-value implementation as the first milestone.

Do not claim AGI has been achieved.
